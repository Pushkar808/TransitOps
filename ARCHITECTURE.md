# 🏗️ TransitOps System Architecture

## Overview

TransitOps is a smart transport operations management platform designed to digitize fleet management workflows including vehicle management, driver operations, trip dispatching, maintenance tracking, fuel management, expense monitoring, and operational analytics.

The system follows a modular architecture with a clear separation between frontend, backend, database, and business rule layers.

The primary goal of the architecture is to provide:

- Scalability
- Maintainability
- Secure access control
- Automated operational validation
- Real-time fleet visibility
